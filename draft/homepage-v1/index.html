Community Spotlight

Parent AI Symposium 2026

DATE
June 5, 2026
LOCATION
Nova High School, Davie

Broward Powered by AI brought together parents, students, educators, and school leaders for an in-person event focused on understanding how artificial intelligence is transforming education.

The symposium featured keynote presentations, breakout sessions, and direct conversations with district leadership. Families explored both the benefits and concerns around AI, with a main focus on what families need to know about AI in our schools.

"A highlight of the day was students themselves demonstrating how they use AI in their learning, alongside district leaders answering questions directly from parents."
