Student AI Council

Student Voice. Real Impact. Responsible AI.

BCPS students are at the center of our AI journey, sharing ideas, raising important questions, and helping shape the future of artificial intelligence in our schools.

Broward Powered by AI

An 18-member advisory body of BCPS students that advises the Executive AI Steering Committee on AI policy, tools, literacy, safety, accessibility, and the student experience across our District.

Advisory Role

Reports quarterly to the Executive AI Steering Committee.

Meets Regularly

Eight meetings per school year plus an annual design sprint.

Student Led

Consensus driven; student voice at the center of decisions.

Our Structure

18 Student Members

Six students from each geographic region: North, Central, and South.

Grades 6-12

At least 2 middle school and 3 high school students from each region, plus one flexible seat per region.

One-Year Term

Renewable once; designed for continuity with about 50% returning members each year.

Supported by Experts

Non-voting District facilitators from Academics/Innovative Learning and Information & Technology, with support from Legal, Student Services/ESE, Privacy, Research, and Communications.

Our Guiding Principles

Human-Centered Learning

AI strengthens relationships, educator expertise, student agency, creativity, and authentic learning.

Student Benefit and Safety

Recommendations prioritize educational value, age appropriateness, and the physical, emotional, academic, and digital safety of students.

Privacy and Security

We use only approved environments and non-identifiable information, never personally identifiable student information.

Access and Accessibility

AI tools and opportunities should be usable by all students, across abilities, languages, devices, connectivity, and backgrounds.

Transparency and Accountability

Students should understand when AI is used, how it works, its limitations, and how concerns can be raised.

Instructional Integrity

AI use aligns with the Florida B.E.S.T. Standards, curriculum, academic honesty expectations, and teacher goals.

Evidence and Continuous Improvement

We rely on student experience, research, local data, and testing, not popularity or vendor claims.

Future Readiness

We help BCPS prepare students for tomorrow's world in fields like cybersecurity, robotics, data science, extended reality, and quantum computing.

How We Engage

Open & Inclusive

Applications are open districtwide with self- and peer-nominations.

Fair & Transparent Selection

A diverse panel evaluates applicants using clear criteria and a holistic review.

Ethical & Respectful

We listen to all perspectives, avoid product advocacy, and put students first.

Accountable Representation

Members commit to meetings, preparation, follow-through, and communicating barriers early.

What We Deliver

Quarterly Recommendation Memos

Evidence-informed recommendations to the Executive AI Steering Committee.

Tool & Pilot Reviews

Student-centered evaluations focused on learning impact, safety, accessibility, and equity.

Student Listening Findings

What we hear from peers across BCPS to inform decisions.

Annual Student AI Experience Report

A districtwide snapshot of student experiences, insights, and recommendations for the year ahead.
